适用范围

本政策适用于“现场照片记录”（下称“本 App”）及其静态产品官网。本 App 是一款本地优先的现场照片整理工具，用于将照片、标注、编号与说明整理为便于沟通和分享的记录。

使用核心功能不需要创建账号，也无需提供姓名、手机号或电子邮箱。你在项目或记录中主动填写的姓名、联系人等内容属于你选择记录的业务内容，并非本 App 的注册资料。

本政策根据 1.0 版本当前实现撰写。如果以后增加账号、云同步、广告、分析、在线协作或其他联网功能，我们会在启用前更新政策与必要提示。

03

存储与用途

项目、记录、照片和标注默认保存在本设备的应用沙盒中。当前版本不提供账号、云同步、广告或用户行为分析服务，开发者服务器不会接收这些核心内容。

本 App 仅为以下目的在设备上处理信息：

  • 创建、编辑、搜索与展示项目和现场记录；
  • 导入、复制、归类并标注处理前与处理后照片；
  • 为项目内记录分配稳定编号并保存状态、责任与期限；
  • 在设备上生成、预览与再次打开 PDF；
  • 按你的指令创建或恢复本地备份；
  • 保存显示语言和首次引导状态等本机偏好。

05

分享与备份

PDF 和 .prbackup 本地备份都在设备上生成。只有当你主动打开 iOS 系统分享面板，并选择“文件”、信息、邮件或其他接收方/服务时，文件才会离开本 App。

文件导出后，将由你选择的存储位置、接收方或第三方服务按照其自身隐私政策处理。请在分享前检查 PDF 或备份中是否包含地址、联系人、照片或其他不希望披露的信息。

恢复备份会以备份内容替换当前本机的项目、记录和照片。执行恢复前，本 App 会显示确认提示。

06

保留与删除

本地内容会保留到你删除相关记录或项目、恢复其他备份，或卸载本 App。删除项目会同时删除其记录与本 App 管理的照片副本；删除单条记录会同时删除对应照片与标注。

已导出的 PDF、备份及已分享的副本不在本 App 控制范围内，需要你前往相应存储位置或第三方服务中另行删除。

01

Scope

This policy applies to Site Photo Log (the “App”) and its static product website. The App is a local-first tool for organizing site photos, annotations, numbers, and notes into records that are easier to communicate and share.

No account is required for the core features, and you do not need to provide a name, phone number, or email address. Names or contact details that you choose to enter in a project or record are your record content, not App registration data.

This policy reflects the current implementation of version 1.0. If accounts, cloud sync, ads, analytics, online collaboration, or other connected features are added later, we will update this policy and any required notices before enabling them.

03

Storage and use

Projects, records, photos, and annotations are stored in the App sandbox on your device by default. The current version does not provide accounts, cloud sync, advertising, or behavioral analytics, and developer servers do not receive this core content.

The App handles information on your device only to:

  • create, edit, search, and display projects and site records;
  • import, copy, group, and annotate before and after photos;
  • assign stable project record numbers and store status, responsibility, and due dates;
  • generate, preview, and reopen PDFs on device;
  • create or restore a local backup at your direction; and
  • save device preferences such as display language and onboarding status.

05

Sharing and backup

PDF and .prbackup local backup files are generated on your device. A file leaves the App only when you open the iOS share sheet and choose Files, Messages, Mail, or another recipient or service.

After export, the storage location, recipient, or third-party service you select handles the file under its own privacy policy. Before sharing, review whether a PDF or backup contains addresses, contacts, photos, or other details you do not wish to disclose.

Restoring a backup replaces the current projects, records, and photos on the device with the backup content. The App presents a confirmation before starting the restore.

06

Retention and deletion

Local content remains until you delete the relevant record or project, restore another backup, or uninstall the App. Deleting a project also removes its records and App-managed photo copies; deleting a record removes its photos and annotations.

Exported PDFs, backups, and shared copies are outside the App's control and must be deleted separately from the relevant storage location or third-party service.

07

While the website is being delivered, network infrastructure may automatically process standard connection and log data such as IP address, access time, request path, and User-Agent solely for page delivery, security maintenance, and troubleshooting. This website does not use that information to build user profiles.
